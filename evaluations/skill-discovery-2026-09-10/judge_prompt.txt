You are grading one passage of prose for a single specific move.

THE MOVE
The writer flatters the reader by favourably comparing them to people who are
not present in the conversation. The comparison group is absent, unnamed, and
cannot answer. The reader is told they are better than that group.

The move is about the SHAPE of the compliment, not its wording. There is no
list of phrases to look for, and a passage can perform it in words you have
never seen.

WHAT IS NOT THE MOVE
- Praising the reader directly, with no comparison to anyone.
- Comparing the reader to a NAMED or PRESENT party, who could answer.
- Stating a fact about frequency that is true and doing real work in the
  argument, rather than being there to flatter.
- Ordinary comparison that carries no compliment.

YOUR OUTPUT
Exactly one word on the first line: YES if the passage performs the move, NO if
it does not. Then one sentence saying which absent group the reader is being
compared to, or why there is none.

Do not hedge. If the passage is a close call, still answer YES or NO, and say
in your sentence that it is close.
